## 3. Conversation With Target User

**Name:** Tutan Mahajan
**Role:** Household cooking and LPG management
**Date:** 20 November 2026
### Three Things She Said

1. **How she checks LPG:** My mother explained that she estimates the remaining LPG based on her experience, the approximate number of days since the last refill, and how heavy the cylinder feels.

2. **Previous unexpected LPG shortage:** She mentioned that the cylinder has run out unexpectedly in the past because she assumed there was enough gas remaining. This made it difficult to plan the next refill in advance.

3. **What would help her:** A reminder a few days before the expected refill date could help her plan ahead instead of depending entirely on assumptions about the remaining gas.

### One Thing That Surprised Me

I was surprised that even though a cylinder typically lasts around 60 days, its remaining LPG is still difficult to estimate reliably without actual measurements.

### How This Conversation Influenced GasWise

I decided that GasWise should combine the approximate 60-day cylinder lifespan with actual cooking-duration records. Instead of claiming to measure the exact amount of LPG remaining, the tool will provide an approximate refill-planning reminder and clearly explain its limitations.

## Data Status

The cooking records were prepared using information gathered from conversations with my mother about her daily cooking routine, along with direct observations made in our home kitchen on 20 November 2026.

Some records represent cooking activities observed directly, while others are estimated examples based on the usual household cooking pattern described by my mother.

The estimated records are intended for testing and should not be treated as individually observed cooking sessions. Further real-world observations are required to validate the model.
